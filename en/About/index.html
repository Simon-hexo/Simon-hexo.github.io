About TianFoto

I am Tian. I photograph on film, and make time into objects you can keep.

I am Tian. I photograph on film, and I make time into objects you can keep.

I live in Germany, in a place where history and the present sit quietly side by side. There are always a few Polaroid cameras, a few pens and a stack of unfinished letters within reach.

Right now I am preparing the 2026 Global Calendar together with Polaroid photographers from around the world — a piece woven from many viewpoints and personal stories. We also make postcard blind boxes, where each edition is decided by public vote, so that an unexpected connection between an image and a person can happen quietly.

If you pass by, you are welcome to tell me your story — and I hope these photographs and words bring a little light when you need it.

What I believe

A photograph is not only a record. It is evidence that a certain stretch of time was taken seriously.

So everything we make is slow: one page a day, one letter at a time, one print at a time. They do not hurry you. They simply wait quietly until the day you look back.
