Handwritten Letter

One Polaroid photograph and one handwritten letter, sent from Germany — written around what you share.

One Polaroid. One handwritten letter.

A letter that crosses snow, time zones and distance from Germany — like an old friend who has not been seen for a while, quietly sitting down beside you on an afternoon that needed company.

No grand theories inside, just small and honest things: the ups and downs of daily life, the direction of study, choices about work, and the moments when you do not have to be strong. Whatever you are willing to share, I will hold carefully.

A sheet of paper and a pen carry the temperature of somewhere far away. The journey of a letter is also a way of moving closer to each other.

Things we can talk about

  • The tenderness or the tiredness that passes quietly through ordinary days
  • A confusion in your studies, and where the next opening might be
  • Pressure, growth and choices at work — I will be a reliable echo
  • Anxiety and feelings that deserve to be answered, not fixed
  • A blessing for someone far away, or one sentence to your future self
  • The story you have kept for too long and never knew who to tell

About this letter

  • Inside: one Polaroid photograph, one handwritten letter, and sometimes a small random gift
  • From: Germany, as a handwritten letter in an envelope — with wind and a taste of somewhere far away
  • Content: written around what you share; every letter is unique to one person
  • Time: international mail varies; the journey may be long or short, and it is worth waiting for
  • Privacy: your privacy is protected. This correspondence belongs only to us

The random gift might be a sticker, a postcard, a bookmark or a small object — a little light that happened to fall on the way.
