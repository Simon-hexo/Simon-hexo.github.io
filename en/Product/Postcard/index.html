Postcard Blind Box

Shortlisted works chosen by public vote, packed at random in sets of eight. Thick card stock, sized like a classic Polaroid.

You choose what gets printed.

Every edition is shortlisted by public vote. The current edition is 2025.07: eight works are picked at random and packed into one box, keeping the surprise and a little ceremony.

Every edition is a shared choice

Shortlisted works come from an open vote, and the most-voted images are the ones printed. Your vote really decides which photograph ends up in someone's hands.

Size and feel

Printed on thick card stock, sized like a classic Polaroid photograph — easy to carry, to keep on a desk, or to write on and send to someone.

Before you buy

  • Light friction in transit may leave faint marks on the back of the cards. This is normal; please choose accordingly.
