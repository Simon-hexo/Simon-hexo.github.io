One frame a day

Tear off a page each day and you get one 1:1 Polaroid frame. A small piece of joy, saved for that particular date.

The back is part of the work

Every page notes the place, the time it was taken, a thought, and a short note from the photographer. The same city, the same day, and completely different feelings.

Tear it out and send it

You can also tear off the page for a given day, write a few lines and post it to a friend or someone you love — passing the warmth on.
