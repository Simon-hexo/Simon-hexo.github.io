This weekend: Wuhan, Hangzhou, Shanghai — let's meet.

Across three days we hosted a few small gatherings in three cities: an evening walk and conversation, or an afternoon of photographing and learning together.

October 16–18 · Wuhan / Hangzhou / Shanghai — activity recap

① Walk & Talk

Find a road worth walking, take an hour or two, stop and start again, and talk about the stories behind photographs.

We talked about recent life and what we are working on, shared experiences and confusions, and spoke about photography and travel — and about how we see and accept ourselves.

The hope was simple: meet a few new friends, hear some different stories, and feel a little warmth from strangers.

② Photograph Together

A practical session in the city, with ordinary people as models — finding the beauty already around us.

  • Making better photographs of landscapes and architecture
  • Designing natural portraits with atmosphere and story
  • Shooting double exposures
  • What makes a good photograph
  • How we can look at and understand an image

Everyone received at least one portrait taken on the day. A camera was not required, and no prior experience was needed.

About the gathering

Every session was kept small, so there was room to actually talk. Walk & Talk could also be joined online.

Polaroid letters could be ordered in advance, a small number of Super365 calendars were available on the day, and everyone who joined had a black-and-white portrait taken.
